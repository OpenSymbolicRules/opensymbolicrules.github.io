# History

Every Verification report is a point below: the rules exported in the `v1/`
rule sets and their status over ℝ and over ℂ (proved, refuted by a
counterexample, unproved), then the same reports as a table, newest first,
and the rules exported per rule set.

figures are read from the Verification repository at the commits shown, see
[Provenance](/provenance/).
