# History

Every Verification report is a point below: the rules exported in the `v1/`
rule sets and their status over ℝ and over ℂ (proved, refuted by a
counterexample, unproved), and the corrected variants (strengthened variants
of refuted rules, proved or not yet proved); then the same reports as a table,
newest first, and the rules exported per rule set. A second chart follows the
v0.1 source rules of the domain repositories, commit by commit. Drag across a
chart to zoom into any period, and double-click it to see the whole history
again.

A report is added after each batch of the [pipeline](/pipeline/); the
figures are read from the Verification repository at the commits shown, see
[Provenance](/provenance/).
